Build the world. Write the book.

Minstrel Studio is free worldbuilding and writing software for novelists. Keep your world, your maps, your timeline, and your manuscript in one place, on your own computer.

Download for Windows See what it does

Free · No account · No subscription · Early release

Everything a story world needs

Made by a working fiction author, for fiction authors, to keep a big world organized without a pile of disconnected documents.

World pages

Characters, places, factions, magic, history. Write pages that link to each other like your own private wiki.

Custom calendars & timelines

Invent your own calendar, then place events and eras on a timeline that links back to your pages.

Maps with pins

Load your own map, drop resizable pins, and link each pin to the pages it belongs to.

Relationship maps

See how your characters connect. Lines can be drawn automatically from your pages or labeled by hand.

A real manuscript editor

Bold, italic, underline, highlight, find and replace, and comments. A pop-out editor window gives you a second-monitor writing setup with chapter navigation.

Tags & quick notes

Capture an idea fast, tag it, and find it again from any page or note that shares the tag.

Honest word counts

Tracks what you actually typed. Pasted text does not inflate your count unless you want it to.

Publish your world

Export your world as a web wiki and a front page you can put online for readers to explore.

Optional AI, off by default

An AI helper is available only if you turn it on and add your own key. If you never do, it never runs.

Download

Minstrel Studio is an early release for Windows. It installs like any other program and saves your worlds to a Minstrel Studio folder inside your Documents.

Download the latest version

If Windows shows a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen: this is normal for new independent software that is not yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  1. Click the download button. On the page that opens, choose the file ending in Setup.exe.
  2. Open the file once it finishes downloading and follow the installer.
  3. Launch Minstrel Studio from your desktop or Start menu and create your first world.

Mac and Linux versions are not available yet.

Your work stays yours

  • Worlds are saved as files on your own computer, not on someone else's server.
  • No account, no sign-in, no subscription.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to publish a wiki or enable the optional AI link.
